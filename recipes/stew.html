Salmon stew

> Home > Salmon stew

Description

A cultural staple that was previously seen as a meal for the lower class
and the hard-working which since has quickly risen in popularity.
It comes in many different variations, depending on season and region,
and can be customized to fit one's pantry.
A particularly famous stew was made by a gourmet chef shortly after her
fishing trip with navy soldiers.

Ingredients

  • Spring water
  • Salmon fillet
  • Salt-cured meat
  • Milk or Cream
  • Flour
  • Onions
  • Celery
  • Seasoning

Recipe Steps

  1. Finely chop vegetables and cured meat
  2. Add cured meat into a pan and cook until brown and crispy, stir occasionally
  3. Add vegetables, cook until softened and add seasonings, stir occasionally
  4. Add flour and mix until uniformly distributed, cook until no raw flour remains
  5. Move from the pan into a cauldron and pour milk over
  6. Add fish fillet chunks and water to desired consistency
  7. Set a cauldron over a fire and let it simmer
  8. Serve immediately once done
