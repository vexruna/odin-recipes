Candy Apples

> Home > Candy Apples

Description

The name is somewhat of a misnomer but it has stuck and there's no going back.
It's a dessert that originated as an attempt to preserve food and has quickly turned
into a local tradition that appears during celebrations.
It's made with a variety of apples and made in multiple different ways but
the outcome is always something sweet, full of flavor and makes you feel right at home.

Ingredients

  • Apples
  • Honey
  • Nuts

Recipe Steps

  1. Remove skin and slice apples into wedges, crush nuts into small chunks or fine powder
  2. Arrange apples on a tray and cover in honey and sprinkle nuts over them
  3. Place the tray in an oven and remove after 30 minutes
  4. Serve after an hour or when warm to the touch
