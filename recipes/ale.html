Mammoth's Tusk Ale

> Home > Mammoth's Tusk Ale

Description

The ale originated as a folk tale of a ransacked brewery that had secretly
kept a stack of barrels buried in the ground which has been uncovered by a
new settler that planned to restore the place to its former glory.

The ale is recognized for its deep, earthy flavor, which comes from
centuries old tradition of fermentation process which uses
barrels made out of old growth oak buried in mountainous permafrost
that keeps it in ideal climate for years.

Ingredients

  • Ice
  • Skal's brew
  • Mighty Mug of Mammoth's Tusk
  • Green Garnish
  • Redcurrant
  • Cinnamon

Recipe Steps

  1. Drop ice inside the mug and pour Skal's brew over the ice
  2. Use woven twines to attach green garnish to the mug
  3. Add redcurrant and sprinkle cinnamon to taste
